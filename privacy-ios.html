Privacy Policy — Usha: Period Tracker & Safety

Last updated: October 22, 2026

Usha is a women's health and safety app (iPhone, iPad and Android) with a private cycle journal, health awareness guides and an emergency SOS tool. Your privacy is our highest priority.

1. Data we collect

Usha does not collect, transmit, sell or share personal data. There are no user accounts, no servers that receive your data, no analytics and no advertising in the iPhone and iPad app.

2. Data stored on your device

Everything you enter (name, period dates, symptoms, notes, screening records and trusted contacts) is stored only on your device, protected by iOS Data Protection, and is excluded from iCloud and device backups. If you set an app PIN, only a salted hash of it is stored in the iOS Keychain on this device. Deleting Usha or using "Delete all my data" in Settings removes your data permanently. Because there is no backup, losing your device or deleting the app can mean losing your journal.

3. Location

If you use SOS, Usha asks for your location once, only at that moment, to add a map link to a text message that you choose to send. Location is not stored or sent to us.

4. Notifications

Reminders are optional and scheduled locally on your device. Notification text never mentions periods or fertility.

5. Purchases

Optional Usha Plus subscriptions are processed by Apple. We do not receive your payment details. We only learn, on your device, whether your subscription is active.

6. Other services

When you tap an external action you leave the app: calls open the Phone app, text messages open Messages, care-package requests open WhatsApp or Messages, shopping links open Blinkit and education links open NHS pages in your browser. Those services have their own privacy policies.

7. Children

Usha is intended for users aged 13 and above and does not knowingly collect any personal data.

8. Medical disclaimer

Usha provides general wellness information. It is not a medical device and does not provide medical advice, diagnosis or treatment. Cycle estimates are not contraception. Always consult a qualified healthcare professional.

9. Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here with a new date.
