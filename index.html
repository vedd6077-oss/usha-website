Smart period & cycle tracker

A simple calendar with predictions for your next period, fertile window and ovulation day — predictions improve automatically as you log more cycles. Track symptoms like mood, energy, pain and flow.

🎀

Breast health, step by step

A guided 5-step monthly breast self-exam, timed to the right days of your cycle, with clear signs that mean it's time to see a doctor.

🌷

Cervical cancer awareness

Clear, judgment-free information on screening and prevention, made for women who may not otherwise have access to this guidance.

🆘

Emergency SOS

A one-tap emergency button with India's key helpline numbers, built for moments when you need help fast.

Private by design
